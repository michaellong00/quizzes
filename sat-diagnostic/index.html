SAT Practice Diagnostic

Short practice check-ins modeled on the digital SAT. Take English and Math separately, in any order, as many times as you like.

English (Reading and Writing)

  • 14 questions · suggested time about 16 minutes.
  • Each question has a short passage (or a pair of short texts) and one multiple-choice question (A–D).
  • No penalty for guessing, so answer every question. Questions get harder as the section goes on.
  • The timer is optional and only warns you; it never locks your answers. It pauses if you switch tabs. Your answers save in this browser as you go.

Done with English? Check that you've answered every question, then submit.

Math

  • 12 questions · suggested time about 16 minutes. A calculator is allowed for every question. Have scratch paper ready.
  • Most questions are multiple choice (A–D). For student-produced response questions, type your answer. Fractions like 7/2 and decimals like 3.5 are both fine.
  • No penalty for guessing, so answer every question. Questions get harder as the section goes on.
  • The timer is optional and only warns you; it never locks your answers. It pauses if you switch tabs. Your answers save in this browser as you go.

Done with Math? Check that you've answered every question, then submit.

This is an informal diagnostic to find a starting point. It is not an official SAT and does not predict an SAT score.
