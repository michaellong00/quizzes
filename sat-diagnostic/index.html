Instructions
  • Section 1: Reading and Writing has 14 questions. Suggested time: about 16 minutes. Each question has a short passage (or a pair of short texts) and one question.
  • Section 2: Math has 12 questions. Suggested time: about 16 minutes. A calculator is allowed for every math question. Have scratch paper ready.
  • Most questions are multiple choice (A–D). For student-produced response questions, type your answer. Fractions like 7/2 and decimals like 3.5 are both fine.
  • There's no penalty for guessing, so answer every question. If you get stuck, make your best guess and move on.
  • The questions get harder as each section goes on, so don't worry if the last few feel tough.
  • The timer is optional and only gives warnings. It never locks your answers. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go.
  • When you finish both sections, click Submit at the bottom to see your results.

This is an informal diagnostic to find a starting point. It is not an official SAT and does not predict an SAT score.
